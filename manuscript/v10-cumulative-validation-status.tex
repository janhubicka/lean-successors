\todo[inline]{\textbf{v10 review: admissible type forest -- GREEN in
the normalized finite unary/binary representation.}
The first missing $E$-coordinate exists uniquely and equals
$\mathrm{fl}(v)$; the exact $E$-cut satisfies the partial-structure
spacing/downward conditions. An admissible partial type of free
level $d$ can be realised after restriction to any earlier cut:
insert one allowed neutral filler vertex at $d$, move the type
vertex to $d+1$, copy the entire induced $L$-reduct on the old
socle plus distinguished vertex, and prescribe exactly the
initial $E$-socle of length $d$. The resulting structure satisfies
all three partial-structure axioms and avoids the forbidden family:
nontrivial irreducible forbidden copies cannot contain the
isolated filler, and non-neutral forbidden singletons are
excluded there. See
\texttt{PrefixReplicaPartial.lean},
\texttt{PrefixReplicaAge.lean}, and
\texttt{AdmissibleKptPrefix.lean}. Finiteness of every level and
the genuine meet and predecessor operations of the admissible
prefix subforest are proved by
\texttt{FiniteKptLevels.lean},
\texttt{AdmissibleKptMeet.lean}, and
\texttt{AdmissibleKptLevelTree.lean}.
This is \emph{not} a proof of the concrete $S$-operation or
monoid axioms in Proposition~6.32.}
