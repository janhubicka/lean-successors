For a finite language with unary and binary relations, a finite
family of enumerated irreducible forbidden structures, no empty
forbidden configuration and an allowed neutral singleton, the
entire prefix-closure problem can be reduced to one explicit
one-filler construction. A forbidden pattern of size at least
two cannot use the filler because it is binary-irreducible;
the remaining vertices project in order, preserving all
present and absent $L$-relations, into the original structure.
A forbidden singleton cannot use the filler because its
singleton type is not neutral. The $E$-spacing and downward
closure, the irreducible-pair $E$ condition, the new free
level $d$, and equality of all $L^+$-type records are checked
by the preceding replica modules.
The resulting \texttt{AdmissibleKptPrefix.lean} formalises
prefix closure of the family of all free-level types of
forbidden-free enumerated partial structures under these
explicit normalization hypotheses (pending exact CI).

LevelTree statement is to establish finiteness of its
