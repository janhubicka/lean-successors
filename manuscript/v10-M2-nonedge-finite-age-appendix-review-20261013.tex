\section*{Appendix review: non-edge finite-age argument for (M2)}

\paragraph{The one missing level.}
Let $F$ be a genuine shape-preserving map and let $q_0\prec a_0$
be consecutive source nodes across a genuine image gap. Put
\[
 n=\ell(a_0),\qquad m=\ell(F(a_0))-1,\qquad
 \lambda(i)=\widetilde F(i).
\]
In particular $\lambda(n-1)<m$. Intrinsic deletion of image
coordinate $m$ gives a genuine shape-preserving map $D$. The checked
level formula and injectivity of $D$ give the uniquely defined partial
restoration $R(D(x))=F(x)$ for every source node $x$.

\paragraph{B1 and B2.}
At the boundary $\ell(x)=n$ prescribe $f(D(x))=F(x)$.
This is unambiguous because $D$ is injective. The complete
last-coordinate deletion identity gives
\[
 f(D(x))\restriction_m=D(x),
\]
including negative directed, singleton, diagonal and $E$ data.
For B2 suppose $D(x)$ and $D(y)$ have the same complete ordinary
$L^+$-socle. Record recovery through $D$ reflects their common
ordinary source socle. The joint padded non-edge witness for two
compatible source types shows that $F(x)$ and $F(y)$ have compatible
ordinary image socles. The distinguished vertices are not required
to coincide. The latter reflection/transport statements are checked
in \texttt{M2ShapeMapCompatReflection.lean} and
\texttt{M2B2Transfer.lean} (merged PRs 357 and 358).

\paragraph{The precise finite B3 age test.}
Let $W$ be an arbitrary finite ordered $L$-age model, with common
prescribed socle through $m$, upper types from one complete
ordinary-compatible prescribed family, and
$W\setminus\{m\}$ forbidden-free. The age model $W$ carries
\emph{no} auxiliary $E$ relation.
If a forbidden irreducible $A$ occurs in $W$, its ordered induced
copy contains $m$. Enumerate the forbidden copy as
$e(0)<\cdots<e(r-1)$. It is enough to retain the finitely many
upper vertices $e(a)>m$.

\paragraph{Step 1: pull back the actual upper graph, and only then pad.}
Use real source coordinates $i<n$ with target address $\lambda(i)$
and real upper coordinates $n+a$ with address $e(a)$ whenever
$e(a)>m$. Pull back \emph{every} $L$ atom from
$W\setminus\{m\}$ to the selected coordinates. All unused source
positions are made neutral and $L$-isolated. This sparse pullback
is order-preserving on its real carrier. It remains
forbidden-free since a forbidden copy on real vertices embeds
inducedly into $W\setminus\{m\}$, while a nontrivial irreducible
forbidden copy cannot meet a fresh isolated filler; normalized
singletons cannot meet a neutral filler.

A vital distinction: the chosen upper vertices retain their
\emph{actual mutual} directed edges and non-edges from $W$.
It would be incorrect to replace all such upper--upper pairs by
non-edges. Only the newly inserted padding vertices are isolated.
The dense source model and its forbidden-age proof are checked
in \texttt{M2SparsePullbackAge.lean} and
\texttt{M2BoundedCopySourceAge.lean}. The explicit binary
(ordinary--upper and upper--upper), unary and diagonal real-vertex
identities are isolated in \texttt{M2BoundedCopyRecords.lean};
this new module needs exact-head CI and named axiom auditing.

\paragraph{Step 2: attach one compatible source E-socle.}
For every chosen upper target vertex take an admissible source
type $T_a$ whose prescribed image has its complete target $L$-type.
By full retained-record recovery, the old-coordinate $L$-record
of $T_a$ agrees with the sparse pullback. The common image socle
and the B2 compatibility reflection supply one source $E$-socle.
Interleave $L$-isolated spacer vertices and give the real upper
vertices the maximal permissible auxiliary $E$-columns. The
non-edge padding constructor preserves the induced $L$-age and
realizes each whole $T_a$ (including the $E$-record) at its
designated position. This construction is on the \emph{source}
and never asserts that the arbitrary test $W$ has an E-expansion.

\paragraph{Step 3: map a single cap, and recover the forbidden copy.}
Add a fresh neutral distinguished type vertex with a complete
high E-cut, obtaining one admissible containing node $C$.
Apply $F$ to this one node. Genuine canonical parameter-prefix
transport identifies the complete image ordinary type of each
chosen upper vertex inside $F(C)$ with $F(T_a)$, supplying
its incoming and outgoing incidences with every target
coordinate at most $m$. In particular, lower coordinates not
belonging to $\lambda[\{0,\ldots,n-1\}]$ are supplied by the
\emph{common complete image socle}; one must not invent
source preimages for them. Retained ordinary--ordinary pair
transport inside $C$ gives the complete upper--upper record,
which equals the record of $W$ by Step 1. Check unary and
diagonal facts for the selected vertices and coordinate $m$.
It follows that the ordered forbidden copy in $W$ appears
inducedly among the corresponding ordinary coordinates in
the forbidden-free node $F(C)$, a contradiction.

\paragraph{Factorization once the outstanding step is checked.}
The compatible-class boring completion gives a genuine global
one-gap map $G$ skipping only $m$ from B1, B2 and compatible B3.
The checked theorem
\texttt{uniformGapDeletedShapeMap\_factorization\_of\_compatibleB3}
then yields
\[
 G(D(x))=F(x)\qquad\text{for every } \ell(x)\le n.
\]
Thus the main remaining work is the simultaneous finite-age
comparison and packaging specified above; neither may be
hidden in an axiom or replaced by the one-linked-upper lemma.
