For a partial structure satisfying the literal $E$-spacing
and downward-closure axioms, a newly inserted ordinary
vertex $\ell$ has a uniquely determined free level
$f=\mathrm{fl}(\ell)$. Its canonical parameter list is
empty exactly for $f=0$, and otherwise consists of the
single full partial type of that vertex over its
$E$-socle of length $f$. Whenever $f>0$, the spacing
axiom implies the \emph{strict} inequality $f<\ell$,
which is precisely the parameter-level requirement
in (S1). The resulting parameter is an admissible
partial type whenever the ambient structure avoids
the forbidden family. See
\texttt{CanonicalInsertionParameter.lean}
(pending exact-head Lean verification).
This is the canonical-decomposition \emph{input}
to Definition~6.30, not yet a proof of the complete
forward successor operation: its no-new-tuples
construction, the equality and uniqueness of
successor representations (S2), and the realization
of every cover (S3) remain to be checked in the
actual normalized $K^\mathcal F_{pt}$ instance.}
