For $n\geq1$, let $V_n$ be the finite set of valid $G^+$ codes of size $n$.
Its finiteness follows from its inclusion in the finite type of all
unary, diagonal, directed binary and $E$ tables of that size. The
canonical code of $H^+\restriction_n$ witnesses $V_n\ne\varnothing$;
no separate neutral-structure construction is needed. Choose a bijective
numbering of each $V_n$ and concatenate these finite numberings in
increasing $n$. The cumulative offsets prove both injectivity and
surjectivity of this enumeration, together with nondecreasing sizes.
These obligations are implemented in \texttt{FiniteLayerEnumeration.lean}
and \texttt{GPlusSizeEnumeration.lean}; countability alone is not substituted
for the enumeration statement. The checked forbidden-age theorem then
applies to this actual enumeration.

$v\mapsto\operatorname{code}(H^+\restriction_{v+1})$ with the inverse
enumeration. The resulting ranks strictly increase, since nondecreasing
sizes would otherwise contradict the strict increase from $u+1$ to $v+1$.
The inverse identities transport the complete induced atomic records,
including absent and reversed relations, into the actual $G^\omega$.
