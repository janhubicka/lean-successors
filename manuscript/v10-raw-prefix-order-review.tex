lengths, initial-socle restriction is now a genuine partial
order, with composition and identity laws, comparable
predecessors below a common record, and a unique
predecessor on each smaller level. The proof handles all
$L$-atoms and the auxiliary $E$-relation and is in
\texttt{RawPrefixOrder.lean}. Together with the concrete
greatest common prefix of two types extracted from one
ambient structure (\texttt{RawTypeMeet.lean}) and the
first-difference level identification
(\texttt{RawMeetLevel.lean}), this checks the local
raw-type meet geometry. To obtain the \emph{actual}
$K^\mathcal F_{pt}$ forest instance one must still prove
that the admissible forbidden-free family is closed
under these induced prefixes, that its levels are finite,
and that the abstract tree order is represented faithfully.
Do not infer that every unrestricted raw $L^+$ record
is an admissible partial type.}
