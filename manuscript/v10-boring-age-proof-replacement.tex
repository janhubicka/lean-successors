To verify that $\type{Q}\in\KFpt$, it remains to check that
		its $L$-reduct avoids $\mathcal F$; the partial-structure axioms
		for $E$ follow from the transported free levels and the requirement
		that every $L$-linked pair belongs to $E$.
		Deleting the inserted ordinary vertex $\ell$ and renumbering gives
		$\type{T}$, so every forbidden copy in $\str{Q}$ must contain $\ell$.
		If no element of $S$ is compatible with $\type{T}|_\ell$, the inserted
		vertex has the allowed neutral singleton type and is $L$-isolated.
		An irreducible forbidden structure cannot contain it, and neither
		can a forbidden non-neutral singleton.

		Otherwise fix $\type{S}_0\in S$ compatible with
		$\type{T}|_\ell$. Let $g$ be a forbidden ordered copy in $\str{Q}$
		containing $\ell$. Take the $L$-structure $\str{A}$ induced on
		the first $\ell+1$ ordinary vertices together with the vertices
		of $g[F]$ above $\ell$ (regarding $t$, if present, as the largest
		ordinary vertex). The domain is a finite ordered subset of $\omega$;
		order-preserving renumbering of the upper vertices does not change
		any induced types.
		By~\ref{item:boringB1} and~\ref{item:boringB2}, its initial
		$L$-socle is
		$\str{f(\type{S}_0)}\restriction_{\ell+1}$.
		Every selected vertex $x>\ell$ belongs to the forbidden copy and,
		by irreducibility, is $L$-linked to $\ell$.
		The construction therefore supplies its complete $L$-type
		through $\ell+1$ from some element of $f[S]$,
		including unary, diagonal, and both directed binary relations
		and their complements.
		Finally, $\str{A}\restriction_{A\setminus\{\ell\}}$ is
		$\mathcal F$-free, since all its atoms occur in the source
		$\str{T}$. Condition~\ref{item:boringage} implies that
		$\str{A}$ is $\mathcal F$-free, contradicting $g$.
