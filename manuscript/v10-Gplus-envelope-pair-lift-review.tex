\texttt{GPlusEnvelopePairLift.lean} uses the already verified
\texttt{ShapeMap.reflects\_le} theorem. If both endpoints $S<T$
of a genuine Kpt representing pair lie in the range of an actual
Kpt ShapeMap $F$, their preimages $s,t$ satisfy $s<t$.

verified representative-independence equation identifies its
$F$-image with $v(S,T)$. Thus any Kpt envelope containing BOTH
endpoints of a representing pair yields the corresponding actual
$G^+$ vertex in the induced-image range. This is extended to
finite families of represented pairs.

covers an arbitrary target pair: the precise seed choice, E1
