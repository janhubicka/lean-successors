\todo[inline]{\textbf{v10 M2: injectivity from the
weak-successor axioms -- PENDING Lean.}
A potential substantial simplification of the level-removal
map is to prove injectivity \emph{after} its level map,
root extension and weak-successor clause, rather than
reconstructing each deleted L/E column individually.
The proposed generic S-tree theorem
\texttt{injective\_of\_level\_order\_and\_weak\_successors}
requires only preservation of equal levels, strict
preservation of the order of source levels, the literal
weak successor property, and $a\leq f(a)$ at level zero.
It uses induction on the source level. Two nodes with
the same image have equal-level mapped predecessors
below that common image, hence equal predecessors by
induction. Their genuine mapped S-step witnesses are
immediate successors on the same level below the common
image, and therefore equal. S2 then recovers the mapped
parameter lists and terminal letters; injectivity at
strictly smaller levels recovers the original parameters
and S2 identifies the original successors. At level zero,
root extension and the forest order already rule out
collapse of distinct roots.

This generic argument introduces no M2 axiom and would
make the explicit injectivity check in the eventual
deleted map a consequence of its other shape axioms.
It does \emph{not} itself prove level preservation,
strict level monotonicity or weak successor
preservation for that map. The latter, particularly
the crossing edge, remains essential.}
