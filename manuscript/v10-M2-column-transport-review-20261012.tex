For a complete successor $L^+$ record at cut $\ell+2$,
assume its last ordinary vertex has a valid canonical E-column
with free cut $f\leq\ell+1$. Deleting any prior ordinary
coordinate $m<\ell+1$ replaces this by the exact cut
$\operatorname{deleteAtCut}(m,f)$, equal to $f-1$
if $m<f$ and to $f$ otherwise. The proposed theorem
\texttt{validNewOrdinaryColumn\_deleteOrdinary}
checks directly that the shortened record satisfies all
three axioms of a valid new ordinary column: initial-segment
E-incidences, absence of reverse E-pairs, and the absence
of either directed binary tuple beyond the transported cut.
No free-level or compatibility assumption beyond the original
validity certificate is needed.

This and the separate exact terminal-letter and
canonical-parameter transport lemmas are prerequisites
for the above-gap weak successor argument. They do not
themselves produce the new admissible successor with the
